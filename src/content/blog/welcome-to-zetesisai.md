# Welcome to Zetesis AI

We are thrilled to announce the official launch of the **Zetesis AI** portal!

## Who We Are

The name **Zetesis** originates from the Greek *ζήτησις*, symbolizing relentless inquiry, systematic exploration, and pursuit of truth. Our objective is to design transparent, reliable, and deeply capable AI architectures that empower developers and researchers.

## What to Expect

1. **Open Source Tools**: Frameworks and libraries dedicated to agent coordination and evaluation.
2. **Technical Articles**: In-depth architecture write-ups, benchmarking reports, and design philosophies.
3. **Community Collaboration**: Open discussions, RFCs, and collaborative projects on GitHub.

Stay tuned as we continue rolling out exciting updates and tools!
